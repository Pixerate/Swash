Insert blocks on iPhone and iPad: a "+" in the keyboard bar opens an Insert panel in place of the keyboard, with tables, callouts, code blocks, math, Mermaid diagrams and images. Pausing on an empty line in Edit Text also shows a small "+" at the caret that opens the panel for that line (announced to VoiceOver, and available as the text view's Insert Block action). The block replaces a blank line, or goes below a line with text, with blank lines around it so the paragraphs on either side stay as they were. The "+" doesn't appear inside code, math or front matter, in Slack mrkdwn, or while distraction-free, and can be turned off with Empty-Line Insert Button in Swash's settings or the Settings app (Refs #194).
