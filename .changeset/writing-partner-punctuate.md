Writing partner, first part (Mac): turn on "Fix punctuation as I type" in Settings › Writing and Swash fixes small things as you finish each word: `i` → `I`, missing apostrophes (`dont` → `don't`), a capital to start a sentence, and a full stop or question mark when you leave a blank line after a one-line paragraph. Each fix is its own undo step; undoing a kind of fix twice in a row stops it, and Settings shows what it has learned. Code, math, HTML, links' addresses and tables are never changed, and it all runs on your Mac. Off by default.
