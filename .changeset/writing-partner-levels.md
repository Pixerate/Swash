Writing partner levels (#229): Off, Copyedit (the default) and Ghostwrite replace Off, Suggest and Automatically, with Suggestions as its own switch for fixes the partner isn't sure of. Spelling, grammar and formatting rules no longer have switches: they're always on and learn from undo, with Curly quotes kept under Typography. Copyedit turns a standalone title line into a heading, Ghostwrite asks Pixerate to rewrite prose in place when you pause, and Edit › Writing Partner Edits lists the partner's Copyedit and Ghostwrite edits this session so you can revert one edit or a whole layer without undoing your typing. Settings on Mac and iPhone/iPad share one five-section layout, and the Partner style preview, now on iOS too, shows the chosen level.
