iPhone Duo: opening or closing the phone now turns the words over. As the hinge moves, each visible word glides to where the other display will show it and flips, so the page becomes the mirror image of the layout about to appear, gathered on the left half where the outer display sits behind the inner one. Opening plays it the other way round. The words follow the hinge, go back if the phone is stood half open or returned, and stay still with Reduce Motion. This replaces the page fold, which never played: the view that watched the hinge was rebuilt during the switch and lost its snapshot.
