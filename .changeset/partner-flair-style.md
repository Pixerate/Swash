Writing partner: its fixes are now drawn with Flair's text treatments, and there's a new look. When a fix lands, a thin ribbon sweeps under the changed word and curls up past its end, then dissolves (a soft lilac in Dark Mode). Settings › Writing has a Partner style picker with a live preview, so you can go back to the soft sweep or choose another style. The partner's cursor has no name tag, and a word it's about to fix is no longer swept first and then selected, which looked like the same highlight twice. Under Reduce Motion every style holds still and fades. Swash now uses Pixerate's editor package 0.73 and Flair 0.135.
