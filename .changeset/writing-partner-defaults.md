Writing partner: "Correct spelling" is now on by default whenever the partner is on, rather than following macOS's "Correct spelling automatically". The partner also fixes more as you type: `ok` → `OK`, `id like` → `I'd like` and `ill be` → `I'll be` (but not "the id field" or "I feel ill"), and a comma before "but" when it starts a new clause ("It works, but it's slow"). Each of these is a rule you can turn off in Settings › Writing.
