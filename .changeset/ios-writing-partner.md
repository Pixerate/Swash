Adds the writing partner to iPhone and iPad. It fixes punctuation, capitals and spelling as you type, in Edit Text and in Source, and each fix is its own undo step that the partner learns from. Suggest mode, marks, the Formatting voice and the pause tier (Pixerate with Swash Pro, or Apple Intelligence on the device) work as on the Mac. Settings › Writing Partner has the same switches as the Mac, and the Writing menu can pause the partner for one document. Suggestions are accepted from the edit menu, or with Tab and Esc on a keyboard. While the partner is on, it turns off the keyboard's own autocorrect, which would fight its fixes. If Pixerate can't be reached within a few seconds, the on-device partner takes the pause, and Pixerate is tried again at the next pause.
