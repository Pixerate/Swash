The editor's list markers are now native SF Symbols on iOS and macOS: task checkboxes are Reminders-style circles (`circle`, and `checkmark.circle.fill` in the accent colour when ticked), and bullets use `circle.fill`, `circle` and `square.fill` by level. Markers take their size from the line's font, stay centred on the item's first line (empty items included), keep 3:1 contrast (using the label colour under Increase Contrast) and fade in Focus Mode. Numbered items use monospaced digits. On the Mac, a task's checkbox is now a circle instead of a checkbox button; clicking it still ticks the task.
