On iPhone and iPad, Edit Text now draws task checkboxes and list bullets as vector shapes that scale with Dynamic Type and line up with the item's text, in colours that keep at least 3:1 contrast with the page. An empty `- ` or `- [ ] ` item at the end of a document keeps its marker centred on its own line instead of drawing it over the line above, and the checkbox's tap target is worked out from the same geometry it's drawn with.
