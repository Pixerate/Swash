Improve Writing Partner terminal punctuation to inspect the final sentence on multi-sentence lines and disambiguate declarative "When"/"Where" subordinate clauses from questions. Commands after the comma, such as "When in doubt, do the simplest thing" or "Where possible, have the build run tests", now end with a full stop; "do", "don't" and "have" after the comma open a question only when a subject follows them ("When you get there, do you call me?").
