Swash has a website: swash.pixerate.com, built from the new `site/` folder and published automatically from `main`. It follows your system's light or dark appearance, and its screenshots come straight from the app. In the app itself, the `-appearance light|dark` launch argument now fixes the appearance for one launch, which the screenshot scripts use (the older arguments stopped forcing Dark Mode in macOS 27).
