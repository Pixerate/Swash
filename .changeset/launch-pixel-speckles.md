The iPhone and iPad document manager now has faint pixel speckles around the "Swash" wordmark: Swash's rounded pixel squares in the brand's pink-to-indigo gradient, twinkling slowly and kept clear of the wordmark and the Create Document button. They use Flair's new `pixel-speckles` effect (FlairEffects 0.145.0), follow light and dark mode, and hold still under Reduce Motion. The wordmark's ribbon no longer draws squashed if its view is resized.
