Writing partner: a limited on-device tier with Apple Intelligence (macOS 26 and later). When you pause and Pixerate isn't answering (signed out, or on the Basic plan), Apple's on-device model reads the paragraph you just finished, holds back punctuation where it would be wrong (a poem, a list of fragments), and may suggest that some lines are a list or a heading. Nothing leaves your Mac, and it reads only that paragraph.
