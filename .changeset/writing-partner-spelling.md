Writing partner: "Correct spelling" (Settings › Writing). While the partner is on, it does the autocorrecting in place of macOS's own: misspellings are fixed the way the system's spell checker would, through the partner's cursor and as part of the same undo step. Undoing a spelling fix leaves that word alone in the document. The switch starts out matching your Mac's "Correct spelling automatically". macOS text replacements keep working, and the partner still adds its capital or apostrophe when a replacement got there first (`teh` → `The`).
