Swash is ready for iPhone Duo (#65). Opening or closing the phone (or rotating a Pro Max, or resizing an iPad window) now keeps the editor's caret, scroll position, keyboard and undo history in every mode, and closing it in Split mode falls back to the last single-pane mode (Source or Formatted) instead of always Source. The view-mode control is the last toolbar item to overflow, and Flavor, Settings, Document Info and Writing the first. The Share popover points at the Share button wherever the bar is, and Settings keeps a horizontal bar. Builds now ship from Xcode 27.1 or later. Opening or closing the phone now keeps the same text at the top of the editor when the lines rewrap, the old page folds into the new layout with a Flair mirror fold (a fade with Reduce Motion), and the "/" menu closes and edits are saved as the hinge starts to move. In Split on the inner display, the divider sits on the fold.
