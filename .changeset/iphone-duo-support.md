Swash is ready for iPhone Duo (#65). Opening or closing the phone (or rotating a Pro Max, or resizing an iPad window) now keeps the editor's caret, scroll position, keyboard and undo history in every mode, and closing it in Split mode falls back to the last single-pane mode (Source or Formatted) instead of always Source. On iOS 27.1, Split uses a split arrangement that keeps the panes on either side of the fold. The view-mode control is the last toolbar item to overflow, and Flavor, Settings, Document Info and Writing the first. The Share popover points at the Share button wherever the bar is, and Settings keeps a horizontal bar. Builds now ship from Xcode 27.1 or later.
