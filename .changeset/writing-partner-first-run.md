Writing partner: on by default for new installs, introduced in place the first time you edit a document with a sample fix and a choice of Automatically, Suggest or Not now. The partner does nothing until that introduction is answered, and when you're signed in it says that the paragraph you pause on is sent to Pixerate. Upgrading keeps your setting (a partner that was off stays off and isn't asked again). Pause it for the current document with Edit › Pause Writing Partner (⌥⌘P); the first time you undo one of its fixes, a quiet reminder says how.
