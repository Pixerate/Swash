Writing partner: curly quotes, dashes and ellipses (Settings › Writing › Typography). In prose only, never in code, math or links: straight quotes and apostrophes become curly, `--` and `---` between words become en and em dashes, and `...` becomes an ellipsis. Each is off unless you turn it on, because it changes the characters in your Markdown file.
