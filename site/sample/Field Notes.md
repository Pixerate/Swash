# Field Notes

Notes from a week of writing without looking back.

## The idea

Most of the work in a document isn't the writing. It's the **tidying**: the capital you skipped, the apostrophe you meant to add, the full stop you never typed. Swash follows a few words behind and quietly does that part for you.

> [!NOTE]
> Every fix is its own undo step. Press ⌘Z and it's gone, and Swash learns not to do it again.

## What I noticed

- I stopped going back to fix things.
- Drafts read like second drafts.
- The files are still *plain Markdown*, so they open anywhere.
